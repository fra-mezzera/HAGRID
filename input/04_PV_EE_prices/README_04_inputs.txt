MODULE 03 FILE DESCRIPTION - INPUT PV & ELECTRICITY PRICE TIMESERIES

	In this folder the hourly timeseries (referred to 2023) for PV generation and the Electricity purchasing price in Italy are presented.

	- For PV, hourly profiles are extracted for a specific case study in Central Italy, starting from renewablesninja webtool
		
		https://www.renewables.ninja/


	- For Electricity price, data refer to the Italian National Single Price (provided by the Italian Energy Market Operator (GME))
