MODULE 03 FILE DESCRIPTION - ADDITIONAL LOAD CONSUMPTION PROFILES PICTURES COUPLED WITH CORRESPONDING ACTIVITY PROFILES


	- In this folder an example of generated pictures is presented. It is possible to obtain graphs that compares the occurring activity profiles by all household members in the tested residential local energy system and the corresponding overall electricity consumption.

	- the electricity consumption is divided into conventional (i.e. appliances and lighting) and additional loads (space heating, cooking, EVs charging, space cooling, domestic hot water demand)

	- the possibility to print a graph with just the stacked electricity consumption within the tested system
